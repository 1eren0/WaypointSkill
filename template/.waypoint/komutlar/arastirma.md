# Research before planning

Runs at stage 2 (and before a big new feature), after `sorgula` made clear what I want. `sorgula` asks what I want; this is what you need to learn to build it well.

1. Decide whether you're missing technical knowledge for a good plan: current docs and versions, existing solutions, alternatives, risks. If not, skip this and plan.
2. Check `raporlar/` first (see "Project files"). Research only what's missing; choose the method and depth yourself: short for a simple project, deeper when it's complex or unclear.
3. Write the findings as a report with its five-line summary and sources. Say in one plain line what you found.
4. Make technical choices yourself and record them in "Kararlar" with why. Tell me only what they mean for me in plain words ("telefonda da çalışır ama ilk açılış biraz yavaş olur"). Product choices (what it does, what I see) stay mine.
5. Base the plan on the findings. Research again during the work whenever you hit something you don't know.
