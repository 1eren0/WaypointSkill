# `sorgula` — question an idea until it's clear

Runs automatically at stage 1 (Idea → Goal) and before a big new feature (one that adds something new I'll see or use, or takes more than one task). Skip it for small fixes and tweaks. I can also start it any time by typing `sorgula`.

1. Collect the open questions about the idea. Ask only product questions (what it does, who uses it, what I should see when, what happens in edge cases), never technical ones: technical choices are yours.
2. Ask one question per message, with your recommended answer, so I can just say "evet". Ask a question only once the answers it depends on are settled; start with the basics.
3. If I answer too quickly to something that matters, ask once more in other words.
4. Stop when nothing important is left unanswered, or after about 10 questions. If questions keep coming, the idea is too big: suggest splitting it into smaller parts.
5. Summarize what we settled in 3–5 plain lines and ask me to confirm.
6. Record it: at stage 1 in "Proje hedefi" and the plan; for a feature in its task's "bitti sayılır" line. Only real choices between options go into "Kararlar".
